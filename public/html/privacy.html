Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This page explains what information Froggly collects and how it's used.

1. What's collected

When you register, Froggly stores your email address, display name, username, and password. If you upload a profile picture or posts, those files are stored as well.

2. How it's used

Your information is used only to run the site: logging you in, displaying your posts and profile to other users, and letting you use features like comments and suggestions. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

3. Who can see what

Your username, display name, profile picture, and posts are visible to other users of the site. Your email address is not shown publicly.

4. Where data is stored

Froggly is built on Supabase, a third-party backend provider. Data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, subject to their own security practices.

5. Your control over your data

You can update your display name, username, and profile picture at any time from Settings. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, also from Settings.

6. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach out to the site owner directly.
